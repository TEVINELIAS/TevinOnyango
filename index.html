About

A data scientist who genuinely enjoys the teaching side of the job

I'm Tevin Onyango, a data scientist working across the full analytics stack — from cleaning a messy spreadsheet to shipping a production data pipeline. My academic background sits at the intersection of mathematics, computer science, data science and financial engineering, which shapes how I approach every dataset: rigorously, but never in a way that loses sight of the business question behind it.

Alongside client and curriculum work, I run structured, paid training programs — for individuals building a career in analytics and for teams that need a specific tool mastered fast. My teaching style favors real datasets, worked examples, and building things that actually get used, over slideware.

Whether you want a dashboard built, a model validated, or a skill taught from first principles, I bring the same standard of rigor to both.
